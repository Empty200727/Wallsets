Update 2026-11-10: copies blurred and dull (user's 3840x2160 60 fps anime video, untagged colours)

  AVEncCommonMeanBitRate); Wallsets set none, so 200 kbit/s (libavcodec default) went in, and a driver
  without a quality mode encodes with it. At 1366x768 a 200 kbit/s copy measures sharpness 5.5 against 8.4
  for a normal one (2.7 vs 6.2 at 1440p).

  at 1366x768 sharpness 9.54 / 2nd-percentile luma 36.8 for the original on the GPU against 8.35 / 41.0 for a
  bicubic copy; lanczos gives 8.88 / 40.9 at 768p and is sharper than the GPU at 1080p and 1440p.

  as BT.601 by mpv (tested), dulling oranges and reds of a BT.709 original.

Update 2026-11-10 (2): copies still blurred and duller than the original (same 3840x2160 60 fps video);
the user: "in the original the outline is stepped and blacker, the smaller the screen the clearer"
Findings (frames of the user's video; the player's drawing emulated as mpv --profile=fast does it: YUV->RGB at
source size, then the card's 2-tap bilinear sampling, correct-downscaling=no, linear-downscaling=no):
- The stepped, darker outlines ARE how the player shows the original; lanczos copies mixed outlines with their
  surroundings. Luma of a lanczos copy vs the player's picture of the original (bundled mpv 0.41, FFmpeg
  N-127231): 43.5 dB, up to 46 levels apart on outline pixels. At 1280x720 / 1366x768 the card's sampling is
  aliased (ratio near 3), at 1920x1080 it is a 2x2 average - which is why smaller screens showed it more.
- FFmpeg's fast_bilinear with 8-bit output takes swscale's quick horizontal path (left-aligned, no half-pixel
  centre): 34.8 dB, up to 159 levels apart. With a 16-bit output it takes the centred 2-tap filter: 80.4 dB
  with sws_dither=none (59.2 dB with the default dither), at most 1 level apart from the card's sampling.
- Chroma: 2-tap on the chroma plane gives 49.5 dB RGB vs the player's picture, ideal 4:2:0 of that picture
  48.3 dB - nothing to gain there; 4:2:0 at screen size is the remaining (small) difference.
- Bitrate (x264 veryfast as a stand-in, 1366x768, 30 fps): exact-sampled copy 46.5 dB at 0.08 bit/pixel,
  48.0 at 0.12, 49.1 at 0.16; outlines lighter by 0.57 / 0.41 / 0.34 levels.
Changes: Optimizer.ScaleGraph: framestep, then scale=W:H:flags=fast_bilinear+accurate_rnd,format=yuv420p16le,
scale=flags=accurate_rnd:sws_dither=none,format=yuv420p; no scaling when the copy keeps the original size;
